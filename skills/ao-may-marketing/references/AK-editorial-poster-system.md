# Áo Mây Editorial Poster System — An Khánh (AK)

Source: User-provided creative prompt, 2026-10-30. Originally AK-only; since 2026-11-01 the style (not the palette, canvas or language) is chain-wide, see `editorial-style.md`. The palette and the 1920 / English rules below remain AK-specific. Keep the store's `status: draft` preflight and verified-contact requirements intact: a creative prompt does not authorize generating a poster before store readiness is satisfied. An explicit later user request takes precedence. No logo reconstruction.

## Canvas
Create a **1920 × 1920 px square Facebook post**. Design a premium editorial poster for Áo Mây, a modern Vietnamese neighbourhood laundry and garment care studio. The poster should feel like a collectible independent magazine cover, creative design studio campaign, or boutique café branding. Never look like a traditional laundry advertisement.

## Brand Emotion
Every poster communicates **“A breath of fresh air.”** Evoke warm Vietnamese morning sunlight, naturally sun-dried clothes, soft breeze, fresh linen, calm neighbourhood lifestyle, effortless cleanliness. Even without showing the sun, the audience should feel the warmth.

## Creative Direction
Every poster must be unique. Each design is another issue of the same magazine: same brand, different story, different composition, different visual concept. Never recycle layouts.

## Core Principle
Visuals must directly support the headline. The image should explain the message before anyone reads the copy.
Examples:
- Laundry for bedding → luxurious white bedding.
- Spa towels → folded spa towels, massage bed, essential oils.
- Shoe cleaning → clean sneakers, brushes, shoeboxes, leather shoes.
- Pickup & delivery → bicycle, delivery bag, movement.
- Laundry café → coffee, books, chairs, neighbourhood atmosphere.
Never use generic washing-machine graphics unless central to the story.

## Layout
Swiss Editorial. Large negative space. Asymmetrical composition. Typography dominates; photography supports. Avoid crowded layouts. Leave room to breathe.

## Typography
Typography is the hero. Extra-bold condensed sans-serif, oversized headlines, minimal supporting copy, strong hierarchy. Only the Áo Mây logo uses handwritten script. Never use decorative fonts, 3D effects, or heavy shadows.

## Graphic Language
Use **2–5 purposeful graphic elements**: large yellow colour blocks, receipt-inspired information cards, editorial rules, bold black marker lines, arrows, barcode, circular stamps, masking tape, paper texture, subtle risograph grain, minimal geometric shapes. Remove purely decorative elements. Less is more.

## Illustration
Maximum **one** illustration: simple expressive hand-drawn bold black marker lines with minimal detail, supporting (not dominating) layout. Possible subjects: folding towels, carrying laundry, watering plants, cycling, reading, drinking coffee, walking a dog.

## Photography
Authentic editorial photography, never generic stock-advertisement imagery. Match topic: white towels, luxury bedding, white shirts, clean sneakers, linen textures, spa environment. Natural lighting, soft shadows, warm sunlight, premium lifestyle.

## Colour Palette
Primary: **Butter Yellow #F6D64A; Warm Cream #FFF8ED; Charcoal Black**.
Accent: **Sky Blue; Soft Sage Green**.
Yellow signifies sunshine. Create warmth with light, shadows, paper texture, window light, negative space and natural fabric.

## Copy
Text minimal, **maximum 30% of canvas**. Headline first; supporting copy second; offer third; contact information last. Prioritize readability. Do not invent or imply an offer not confirmed in store profile.

## Brand DNA
Every poster instantly feels like Áo Mây: recognizable, never repetitive. Change layout, image crop, typography scale, graphic system, balance, editorial details, storytelling.

## Avoid
Traditional laundry advertisements, corporate flyers, template layouts, clip art, many icons or illustrations, heavy gradients, 3D effects, kawaii style, cartoons, crowded layouts, long paragraphs, excessive information, generic stock-ad look.

## Final Art Direction
A mood appropriate to Kinfolk, Apartamento, Cereal Magazine, COS campaign, Studio Airport, Midday Studio, Saint Urbain and modern Swiss graphic design; do not copy their copyrighted works or logos. Premium, minimal, warm, sunlit, thoughtful, timeless.

## Absolute Rules
1. Requested composition canvas **1920 × 1920 px (1:1)**. Note: the existing chain skill has a mandatory **1080 × 1080 final export** for DQH-01. This 1920 size is scoped to AK only and does not change DQH-01. If a later specific deliverable requires another output size, follow the user's explicit instruction and verify actual exported dimensions.
2. Use official Áo Mây logo **exactly as provided**, do not redraw, reinterpret, translate or modify; adhere to AK's `chain+label` identity rules.
3. **All poster copy in English unless specific Vietnamese marketing text is explicitly provided.** Verified store contact and proper names remain faithful to the source; do not translate address into fake facts.
4. Maintain generous whitespace; when in doubt remove rather than add.
5. One strong visual idea is better than ten small decorative elements.
6. Preserve the chain's safety/QA gate for verified services, deals, opening hours and store status. This creative brief does not confirm store facts or mark any design approved or published.
