## Chain
Áo Mây is a laundry franchise brand. Chain-level services confirmed: giặt sấy, giặt hấp, vệ sinh giày. Whether a given store offers a service is a store fact (Notion 02 / 04), not implied by the chain list.
Fixed chain-wide: chain logo, palette, font system, voice, prohibitions, poster structure, QC checklist. Store-specific: store code, branch lockup logo (if any), contacts, services actually offered, confirmed offers, store template, local hashtags, real photos.
“Giặt sấy trong vòng 24 giờ” concerns turnaround, never 24/7 opening hours.

## Voice and audience
Vietnamese, friendly, persuasive, local and concise. Start with a varied concrete situation, explain supported benefits, finish with one clear CTA and a few relevant hashtags. Audience descriptions are working hypotheses. Do not fabricate quotes, testimonials, numbers, guarantees, discounts or before/after results.

## Design — chain-wide (route direct)
Proposed tokens (not official brand kit): aqua #36C9D6, teal #087F88, mist #EAFBFC, white #FFFFFF, ink #17383D, sun #FFC857. Approved TH direction: sky blue, dark teal, white, small yellow accents. Be Vietnam Pro for text; 64 px safe margin; few words. Never use Miu Miu identity (pastel pink etc.) for Áo Mây.
Rotate Service Hero, Editorial Split, Local Store, Tips Infographic, Story/Pain Point, Behind the Scenes. Compare with the store's two most recent posters; avoid repeated crop and headline arrangement.

## Design — store templates (route compose)
Each template's parameters live only in that store's spec.json. DQH-01 (approved 26/10/2026): logo top-centre → headline → sub-line → subject → benefit bar → fixed 2-box CTA footer; light teal gradient background, restrained clouds/bubbles, no podium by default; headline uppercase extra-black with white stroke and teal shadow. Not allowed: pink "LIÊN HỆ NGAY" button, merged single contact box, AI-drawn logo or Vietnamese text, rough cutouts.
